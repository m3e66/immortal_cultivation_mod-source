# Immortal Cultivation Mod

Walk the path of cultivation, master divine arts, command countless swords, and seek immortality.

> Immortal Cultivation Mod is a large-scale Minecraft cultivation mod focused on cultivation, realm breakthroughs, spells, cultivation methods, artifact forging, talismans, formations, flying swords, corpse control, NPCs, and world exploration.
> You begin as an ordinary cultivator and gradually walk your own path toward immortality by absorbing Qi, practicing cultivation methods, learning spells, exploring ruins, forging artifacts, engraving Restrictions, and breaking through cultivation realms.
> Many systems are more than simple stat upgrades.
> Realm breakthroughs, forging, Restrictions, talismans, formations, and combat all have their own unique mechanics and gameplay systems.
> The mod is still under active development, so some models, GUIs, balance values, and gameplay mechanics may continue to change.

# 修仙之路

踏仙途，炼神通，御万剑，证长生。

> 这是一个以修仙、境界突破、法术、功法、炼器、符箓、阵法、御剑、控尸、NPC与世界探索为核心的大型修仙模组。
> 玩家将从一名普通修士开始，通过吸收灵气、修炼功法、学习法术、探索遗迹、炼制法器、刻画禁制并突破境界，一步步踏上属于自己的修仙之路。
> 模组中的许多系统并不只是简单的数值提升。
> 境界突破、炼器、禁制、符箓、阵法与战斗都拥有各自独立的玩法与机制。
> 本模组仍处于持续开发阶段，部分模型、GUI、数值与玩法可能会继续调整。
